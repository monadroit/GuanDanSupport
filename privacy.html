中文隐私说明

本政策适用于青竹掼蛋（Qingzhu Guandan）的 iPhone 与 iPad 原生应用，以及本支持网站。应用无需账号，没有中央游戏服务器，也不包含广告、分析或跟踪 SDK。应用不会自动把你的牌局、使用记录或设备标识上传给开发者。

保存在设备上的信息

  • 单人牌局与偏好：当前牌局、升级进度、手牌顺序、尚未完成的还牌，以及音效和速度设置保存在应用的本机存储中。
  • 昵称与本机玩家标识：应用保存你填写的昵称，以及随机生成的玩家标识，用于记住玩家和保护附近座位的归属。它不是广告标识，也不需要真实姓名。
  • 重连凭据：应用将最近最多八个座位的牌桌标识、原座位、归属标识和秘密重连令牌保存在本机 Keychain 中。这些令牌仅供原设备恢复座位，不会交给游戏网页界面；Keychain 项设置为设备解锁时可用、仅限本设备。

这些本机数据不会由应用自动同步到开发者服务器或其他设备。系统备份等 Apple 服务可能按你的设备设置处理普通应用数据；它们独立于本应用的游戏功能。

附近联机中共享的信息

只有在你使用「附近联机」时，应用才查找或创建本地牌桌。房主的牌桌名称（包含所选昵称）会向附近正在查找牌桌的设备公开，加入前也可看到。

加入后，你的昵称、随机玩家标识、座位、身份验证信息和游戏操作会通过加密的本地连接发送给房主。其他同桌玩家可以看到昵称、座位和公开牌局信息，例如已出的牌、剩余张数、贡牌、级数和名次。房主设备掌管完整发牌并验证操作；客人收到自己的手牌和公开信息，每局结束时显示剩余手牌。请与信任的牌友联机。

附近游戏不经过开发者运营的服务器。正在进行的附近牌局保存在房主内存中；房主关闭牌桌或应用后不会作为可恢复的牌局存档保留。保存的重连令牌不包含牌局备份。

权限、复制与分享

「本地网络」权限用于发现和连接附近牌桌。单人练习不需要此权限。应用的游戏功能不请求通讯录、位置、相机、照片或麦克风访问。

只有点击「复制」时，应用才会将邀请内容写入系统剪贴板；不会读取剪贴板。点击「分享」会打开系统分享菜单，由你选择接收者或其他应用。分享后，所选服务会依其自身规则处理内容。

支持邮件

如果你主动联系支持，开发者会收到你的邮件地址、消息内容和你选择发送的附件，用于回复和处理问题。邮件通过相关邮件服务提供商传递，不是应用自动收集的诊断数据。请勿发送不必要的个人信息或仍在使用的座位 PIN。

你的选择与删除

你可以仅使用单人模式，或在系统设置中关闭本地网络权限。可在加入或创建牌桌前修改昵称，清空昵称会删除已保存的昵称并恢复默认名称。「重新开局」会替换当前单人牌局。

删除应用会移除其普通本机数据，但 Apple Keychain 中的凭据可能独立保留，不能保证随卸载一并删除。应用目前没有清空全部 Keychain 座位凭据的按钮；较旧凭据会在缓存超出八个时移除，收到房主已认证的牌桌结束或座位拒绝通知时也会移除对应凭据。退出牌桌或暂时断线本身不会清除凭据。

开发者无法远程读取或删除你设备中的牌局与凭据。若要询问或请求删除你通过支持邮件提供的信息，请使用下方地址联系。

本支持网站与外部服务

这些静态页面由 GitHub Pages 托管，未添加分析脚本、广告或跟踪 Cookie。访问网站时，托管方可能处理 IP 地址、浏览器信息和请求日志等技术数据；详情见 GitHub 隐私声明。打开网站或发送邮件需要相应网络服务，应用的离线游戏不依赖它们。

Apple 的应用分发、设备备份等服务以及你选择的分享或邮件服务，适用各自的隐私政策。

ENGLISH PRIVACY POLICY

This policy covers the native Qingzhu Guandan app for iPhone and iPad and this support website. The app requires no account, has no central game server, and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not automatically upload your games, usage history, or device identifiers to the developer.

Information stored on your device

  • Solo matches and preferences: The current match, level progress, hand arrangement, pending tribute returns, and sound and speed settings are stored locally within the app.
  • Nickname and local player identity: The app saves your chosen nickname and a randomly generated player identifier to recognize you and protect ownership of a nearby seat. This is not an advertising identifier, and your real name is not required.
  • Reconnection credentials: A device-local Keychain cache stores up to eight recent seat credentials: table identifier, original seat, ownership identifier, and a secret reconnect token. Tokens allow the original device to restore its seat and are not exposed to the game's web interface. Keychain entries are configured as accessible when the device is unlocked and restricted to this device.

The app does not automatically sync this local data to developer servers or other devices. Apple services such as system backups may handle ordinary app data according to your device settings; these services are separate from the app's game features.

Information shared during nearby play

The app discovers or creates local tables only when you use Nearby play. The host's table name, which includes their chosen nickname, is advertised to nearby devices looking for tables and is visible before joining.

After joining, your nickname, random player identifier, seat, authentication information, and game actions are sent to the host over encrypted local connections. Other players at the table see names, seats, and public game information such as played cards, remaining card counts, tribute cards, levels, and results. The host device holds the full deal and validates actions. Guests receive their own hand and public information; remaining hands are revealed at the end of a round. Play with people you trust.

Nearby games do not pass through developer-operated servers. An active nearby match lives in the host's memory and is not retained as a recoverable saved match after the host closes the table or app. Stored reconnect tokens do not contain a backup of the game.

Permissions, copying, and sharing

Local Network permission is used to discover and connect to nearby tables. Solo practice does not need it. Game features do not request access to contacts, location, camera, photos, or microphone.

The app writes an invitation to the system clipboard only when you tap Copy; it does not read the clipboard. Share opens the system share sheet, where you choose a recipient or another app. The selected service handles shared content under its own policies.

Support email

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send, to respond and address the issue. Email passes through the relevant email service providers; it is not diagnostic data collected automatically by the app. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information or active seat PINs.

Your choices and deletion

You can play solo only or turn off Local Network permission in system Settings. Edit your nickname before joining or hosting; clearing it removes the saved nickname and restores the default name. Starting a new solo match replaces the current one.

Deleting the app removes its ordinary local data, but Apple Keychain credentials may persist independently and are not guaranteed to be removed with the app. There is currently no in-app button to erase all saved Keychain seat credentials. Older entries are removed when the cache exceeds eight, and an authenticated table-ended or seat-rejection notification from the host removes the corresponding credential. Leaving a table or temporarily disconnecting does not itself clear the credential.

The developer cannot remotely read or delete games or credentials on your device. To ask about or request deletion of information you sent by support email, contact the address below.

This website and external services

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages and add no analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies. When you visit, the hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address, browser information, and request logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening this website or sending email uses the corresponding network services; offline gameplay does not depend on them.

Apple services such as app distribution and device backups, and any sharing or email services you choose, are governed by their own privacy policies.
