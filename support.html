中文帮助

本页适用于青竹掼蛋的 iPhone 与 iPad 原生应用，需要 iOS / iPadOS 17 或更新版本。应用内界面使用简体中文。

联系支持

artful.skates_4j@icloud.com

请说明设备型号、系统版本、应用版本、所用模式，以及出现问题前的操作步骤。截图有助于排查；请遮住不想分享的昵称、PIN 和其他个人信息。

离线单人练习

打开应用，选择「单人练习」即可与三位电脑牌友开局。游戏、规则和电脑策略都已内置；首次打开也不需要联网下载或预先准备。提示和托管在本机运行。

当前单人牌局、理牌顺序和设置会自动保存在本机，重新打开可继续，包括尚未完成的还牌。选择「重新开局」会替换当前单人牌局和升级进度。应用不提供账号或云端同步。

与附近的好友一起玩

  1. 各设备打开 Wi-Fi，建议同时打开蓝牙，并允许青竹掼蛋访问「本地网络」。让设备靠近，使用相同应用版本，保持应用在前台。
  2. 一人进入「附近联机」，填写昵称、选择房主座位并创建牌桌。
  3. 房主将对应座位的四位 PIN 告诉每位好友，也可点击「复制」或「分享」。每人使用自己的 PIN，开头的 0 也要输入。
  4. 好友找到牌桌后输入 PIN，自动坐到该 PIN 对应的座位，无需另选座位。
  5. 房主确认大家就座后开局，空位由电脑补足。对面是队友：南北一队、东西一队。开局后不能再加入新玩家。

附近联机使用设备之间的本地连接，不需要互联网或共享 Wi-Fi 路由器。在飞行模式中使用时，需要重新打开 Wi-Fi。

选牌、理牌与还牌

轻点手牌进行选择，再点击「出牌」；不跟牌时点击「不出」。拖动手牌可调整两行中的顺序，「理牌」可重新排序。配牌有多种合法组法时，请从菜单选择想出的牌型。完整规则可在游戏内查看。

下一局需要进贡时，贡牌自动送出。你收到贡牌后，从高亮手牌中选择一张，再点「确认还牌」。优先还不大于 10 的非级牌、非配牌；没有时可选原手牌中的其他非配牌。刚收到的贡牌不能立即还回去。所有还牌完成后才开始出牌，电脑自动还牌。

附近联机时由房主确认出牌与还牌，并在结算后开始下一局。单人模式的提示和托管不用于附近真人玩家的操作。

断线后回到原座位

玩家暂时离开或断线时，原座位保留，牌局暂停。回到应用会尝试重连。客人重启应用后进入「附近联机」，会自动寻找最近的原牌桌；附近列表中的「回到原座位」也可恢复，无需重新输入 PIN。若暂时找不到房主，可以继续等待、重试，或选择「寻找其他牌桌」。

请让房主保持原牌桌打开

恢复需要同一张牌桌仍在房主设备上运行，且该座位尚未被房主改为电脑。房主退出牌桌、强制关闭应用，或系统终止牌桌页面后，这次附近牌局就会结束，不能从存档恢复。保存的座位凭据只用于重连，不是牌局备份。

房主可以让电脑接替断线玩家，接替后该真人本次不能再回到那个座位。其他人的 PIN 不能用于接管已被原设备占用的座位。

找不到牌桌或无法加入？

  • 在系统设置的「隐私与安全性 → 本地网络」中，确认已允许青竹掼蛋。
  • 确认 Wi-Fi 已打开、设备靠近、双方应用版本相同，房主仍停留在原牌桌。
  • 首次加入时核对完整四位 PIN；恢复原座位时使用「回到原座位」，无需旧 PIN。
  • 保持应用在前台。若一直无法恢复，先确认房主是否已经关闭牌桌或让电脑接替。

昵称保存在本机，下次会自动填入；开桌或加入前可修改。清空昵称会恢复默认名称。有关本机存储、附近连接和支持邮件，请查看隐私政策。

ENGLISH SUPPORT

These instructions cover the native Qingzhu Guandan app for iPhone and iPad, requiring iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. The app interface is in Simplified Chinese.

Contact support

artful.skates_4j@icloud.com

Please include your device model, OS version, app version, game mode, and the steps leading to the issue. Screenshots help; hide names, PINs, and other personal information you do not want to share.

Practice solo, offline

Open the app and choose “单人练习” (Solo practice) to play with three computer players. The game, rules, and computer strategy are bundled in the app. Even the first launch needs no internet download or offline preparation. Hints and autoplay run on your device.

Your current solo match, hand arrangement, and settings save locally. Reopening resumes the match, including an unfinished tribute return. “重新开局” starts a new match and replaces the current solo match and level progress. There is no account or cloud sync.

Play with nearby friends

  1. Turn on Wi-Fi on each device; keeping Bluetooth on is also recommended. Allow Local Network access, keep devices nearby, use the same app version, and keep the app in the foreground.
  2. One player opens “附近联机” (Nearby play), enters a nickname, selects the host seat, and creates a table.
  3. The host gives each friend the four-digit PIN for their seat, using “复制” (Copy) or “分享” (Share) if desired. Each guest needs their own PIN. Include any leading zeroes.
  4. Guests find the table and enter their PIN. The PIN assigns the seat automatically; guests do not select a separate seat.
  5. The host starts once everyone is seated. Computer players fill empty seats. Opposite seats are partners: South/North and East/West. New players cannot join after the game starts.

Nearby play uses local connections between devices. It does not require internet access or a shared Wi-Fi router. If using airplane mode, turn Wi-Fi back on.

Selecting, arranging, and returning cards

Tap cards to select them, then tap “出牌” (Play), or use “不出” (Pass). Drag cards to arrange the two-row hand; “理牌” sorts it again. When wild cards allow multiple legal combinations, choose the intended combination from the menu. Full game rules are available inside the app.

When tribute is due for the next round, tribute cards are sent automatically. If you receive one, choose a highlighted card and tap “确认还牌” (Confirm return). Eligible cards are non-level, non-wild cards no higher than 10; if none are available, another non-wild card from your original hand can be returned. You cannot immediately return the tribute card you just received. Play begins after all returns are complete. Computer players return cards automatically.

In nearby play, the host validates plays and tribute returns, and starts the next round after the result. Solo hints and autoplay do not control nearby human players.

Returning after a disconnection

A temporarily absent or disconnected guest keeps their seat, and the table pauses. Returning to the app attempts reconnection. After restarting the guest app, open Nearby play to search automatically for the most recent table. “回到原座位” (Return to your seat) in the table list also reconnects without a PIN. If the host is temporarily unavailable, wait, retry, or choose “寻找其他牌桌” (Find another table).

The host must keep the original table open

Restoring a seat requires the same table to remain running on the host device and the seat not to have been replaced by a computer player. Leaving the host table, force-quitting the host app, or the system terminating its table page ends the nearby match. It cannot be restored from a saved match. Stored seat credentials support reconnection; they are not a game backup.

The host can replace a disconnected guest with a computer player for the rest of the table. That guest can no longer return to the replaced seat. Another person's PIN cannot take over a seat already owned by its original device.

Cannot find or join a table?

  • In system Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network, allow 青竹掼蛋.
  • Check that Wi-Fi is on, devices are nearby, app versions match, and the host still has the original table open.
  • For a first join, check all four PIN digits. To restore a saved seat, use Return to your seat; the old PIN is not required.
  • Keep the app in the foreground. If restoration keeps failing, check whether the host closed the table or replaced your seat with a computer player.

Your nickname is saved on this device and filled in next time. You can edit it before hosting or joining; clearing it restores the default name. See the privacy policy for local storage, nearby connections, and support email.
